## User Story
As an insurer, I want routine pre-auths decided instantly by versioned rules so that hospitals are not kept waiting.

## Description
Implements AC-04, AC-05 as specified in specs/preauth_spec.md, specs/adjudication_spec.md. The feature spec is the source of truth; this story is the unit of work for one generator teammate.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Estimate <= threshold with auto-approved diagnosis is APPROVED with PA-AUTO-APPROVED.
- Estimate equal to threshold auto-approves; one paisa above stays PENDING.
- Policy cap below estimate yields PARTIALLY_APPROVED at the cap with PA-AUTO-CAPPED.
- Excluded diagnosis is REJECTED by SYSTEM; unknown diagnosis is QUERY_RAISED.
